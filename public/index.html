    Pilgrimages

    Pilgrimage journeys

    Our office is on the road to Erumeli, so Sabarimala is home ground. We meet devotees at the station or the airport, drive them to Nilakkal and bring them home, with one driver for the whole trip.

    Before you travel to Sabarimala
    • Book your darshan slot on the official Sabarimala Virtual Queue portal before you set out.
    • Nilakkal is the base camp. Private vehicles usually park there and KSRTC buses run to Pamba. Rules change each season; we tell you the latest.
    • Carry photo ID and the Virtual Queue printout or screenshot.
